# Hackathon plan — Campus food drive helper

Sprint goal: By Sunday 16:00 a volunteer can find an open shift and sign up with a college email, and an organiser can see which shifts are short.

Capacity: 16 points

Four people for 30 hours, minus set-up, meals and the demo. Our last two hackathons finished 15 and 17 points
of similar stories, so we plan 16.

## MVP

- HACK-4 List open shifts (3)
- HACK-5 Sign up for a shift with a college email (5)
- HACK-6 Cancel my sign-up (2)
- HACK-7 Show drop-off points on a list (2)
- HACK-8 See sign-ups per shift (3)

Total: 15 points, leaving 1 point of slack for the demo build.

## Later

The map, reminders, CSV export and leaderboard (15 points) wait until after the demo. The map was split from
"drop-off points" so the list alone can ship first.
