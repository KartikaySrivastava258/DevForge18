# Hackathon backlog

Your team of four has 30 hours at the campus hackathon to build a helper for the food drive: volunteers sign
up for shifts, donors find drop-off points, organisers see who is coming. At the kick-off you typed the
brainstorm straight into Jira; `export.json` is the result and `plan.md` is still empty.

Turn the brainstorm into a backlog you can actually deliver by the demo:

- Epics for the big goals, each with its stories.
- Stories in "As a …, I want … so that …" form with checkable acceptance criteria.
- Every story labelled `mvp` (must be in the demo) or `later`.
- MVP stories estimated in story points (1, 2, 3, 5 or 8 — split anything bigger).
- `plan.md`: the sprint goal, the team's capacity in points with your reasoning, and the MVP keys. The MVP
  must fit the capacity.

Do the work in your own Jira project, export it with `export.py`, and run `bash check.sh .`.
