# Fees

## Payment deadlines

Tuition fees are paid in two instalments: the first by 15 July and the second by 15 December. A payment made
after the deadline carries a late fee of 100 rupees per week. Students with unpaid fees are not issued a hall
ticket.

## Withdrawal and refunds

A student who withdraws from the programme before classes start gets a full refund of the tuition fee, minus
a processing charge of 1,000 rupees. After classes start, no tuition fee is refunded, but the hostel deposit
is returned in full once the room has been inspected.
