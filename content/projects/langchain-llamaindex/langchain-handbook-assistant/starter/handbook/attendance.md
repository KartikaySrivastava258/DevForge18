# Attendance

## Minimum attendance

Students must attend at least 75 percent of the classes in each course to sit that course's end-semester
examination. Attendance is counted separately for lectures and laboratory classes, and each must reach 75
percent. Faculty publish attendance on the student portal every two weeks; check it and raise any error with
the course teacher within seven days of publication.

## Medical leave

Absence because of illness can be excused with a medical certificate from a registered doctor. Submit the
certificate to the department office within five working days of returning to classes. Excused absence is
not counted against attendance, but a medical certificate submitted late is not accepted.

## Condonation of shortage

A student whose attendance is between 65 and 75 percent in a course may apply for condonation of the
shortage. Condonation is decided by the attendance committee of the department, which meets in the last week
of every semester.

The application must explain the reason for each absence and include any supporting documents, such as
medical certificates that were not submitted earlier, letters from a sports or cultural coordinator for
events where the student represented the college, or proof of a family emergency. Applications without
reasons are not considered.

Condonation is granted at most twice during a programme, and a fee of 500 rupees is charged for each course
that is condoned. A student whose attendance is below 65 percent in a course cannot be condoned and must
repeat the course in a later semester. The committee's decision is published on the student portal before
the examination timetable.
