# Hostel

## Curfew

The hostel gates close at 10 pm on weekdays and at 11 pm on Saturdays and Sundays. A resident who will return
after the curfew must inform the warden in writing before leaving. Repeated late returns are reported to the
resident's parents.

## Guests

Visitors may meet residents in the hostel visitors' lounge between 4 pm and 7 pm. Parents may visit a
resident's room with the warden's permission. Guests may not stay overnight in the hostel.
