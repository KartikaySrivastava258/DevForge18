# Library

## Borrowing books

Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books at a time for fourteen days, and postgraduate students up
to six books for twenty-one days. A loan can be renewed twice at the issue desk or on the library portal,
unless another student has reserved the book. Reference books and journals cannot be borrowed.

## Late returns and lost books

A book returned late is fined two rupees per day for each day it is overdue. A student who loses a book must
replace it with a new copy of the same edition or pay twice its current price. Library fines must be cleared
before the hall ticket is issued.
