# Examinations

## Hall tickets

The examination office issues a hall ticket to every eligible student one week before the end-semester
examinations. Download it from the student portal and bring a printed copy and your college identity card
to every examination. Students without a hall ticket are not admitted to the examination hall.

## Grading

Each course is graded on a ten-point scale: O is 10, A+ is 9, A is 8, B+ is 7, B is 6, C is 5 and F is 0.
The SGPA is the credit-weighted average of the grade points of one semester; the CGPA is the same average over
all semesters so far. A grade of F in a course means the course must be cleared in a supplementary
examination.

## Re-evaluation

A student who disagrees with the marks in an end-semester examination may apply for re-evaluation of the
answer script within ten days of the results. The fee is 750 rupees per course. A different examiner evaluates
the script again, and the new marks replace the old ones whether they are higher or lower.

## Malpractice

Phones, smart watches and any other electronic device are not allowed in the examination hall, even when
switched off. Copying, carrying notes or communicating with another student during an examination is
malpractice. Malpractice cases are reported to the examination committee, which can cancel the examination
in that course or in all courses of the semester.
